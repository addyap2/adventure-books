Branching stories for people learning English, where you are the hero. The same story is written at three levels — so you read at A2, B1, or B2, switch level whenever you like, and still make the same choices as everyone else. Every word waits in your language when you need it.

Pick your level

Read at A2, B1, or B2 — beginner to advanced — and change it any time, even mid-story. Every level follows the same path, so a whole class can read one book together.

02

Read and choose

Every page ends in a decision, and you decide what happens next. Choosing well means understanding what you just read.

03

Tap any word

See its meaning in your own language, right where you are — the English first, your language as the safety net beneath it.
